I'm **Erdiansyah** — a DevOps and software architecture specialist with over
18 years of hands-on experience across software engineering, system
architecture, and technical leadership, based in Jakarta, Indonesia.

My expertise spans cloud-native architectures, CI/CD pipelines,
infrastructure automation, and platform engineering, with a strong
foundation in system design, architectural governance, and configuration
and change management. I routinely bridge development, DevOps, and
infrastructure domains: leading cross-functional engineering teams,
managing complex IT initiatives, and driving enterprise DevOps practices
that improve delivery velocity, operational reliability, and platform
stability.

I believe disciplined execution, continuous improvement, and a strong
commitment to on-time and under-budget delivery are essential to
sustainable success and long-term team growth.

I also care about small, legible tooling. This site is a case in point:
markdown files, one pandoc template, one Makefile — no framework, no
node_modules. Most of what I publish here starts life as a note in my
zettelkasten — shell gotchas, git mental models, infrastructure patterns —
promoted to a post once it has proven useful more than twice.
